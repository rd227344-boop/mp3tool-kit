Free MP3 merger online.

Add your tracks, put them in the order you want, and join them into one MP3. Nothing is uploaded.

    How to merge MP3 files

    1. Choose two or more audio files from your device.
    2. Use the up and down buttons to put them in the right order.
    3. Press Merge and download MP3.

    Questions

    Are my files uploaded anywhere?

    No. Your files are read and joined inside your browser, and they never leave your device.

    Can I change the order of the tracks?

    Yes. Use the up and down buttons next to each track. The merged file plays in the order shown in the list.

    What if my files have different quality?

    That is fine. The tracks are matched to the same sample rate and the result is saved as a 128 kbps MP3, so there can be a small quality difference from a high-quality original.

    How many files can I join?

    Up to 20 files. For smooth results, especially on phones, keep the total under 100 MB.
