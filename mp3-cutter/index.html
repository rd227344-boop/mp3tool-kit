How to cut an MP3

  1. Choose an audio file from your device.
  2. Drag the two handles, or use the arrow keys, to select the part you want. Press Play selection to check it.
  3. Press Cut and download MP3.

Questions

Is my audio uploaded anywhere?

No. The file is decoded and cut inside your browser, and it never leaves your device.

Which formats can I open?

MP3, WAV, M4A and OGG, depending on what your browser supports. The cut clip is always saved as an MP3.

Does cutting reduce the quality?

The clip is re-encoded as a 128 kbps MP3, so there can be a small quality difference from a high-quality original.

Is there a file size limit?

There is no fixed limit, but large files use a lot of memory. Files under 100 MB work best, especially on phones.
